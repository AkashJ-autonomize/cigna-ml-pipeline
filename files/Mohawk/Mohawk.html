On-Site Services:

Mohawk Healthy Life Centers (HLCs) through Evernorth Direct Health

  • Customers make an appointment via text or call 1.877.365.0051. 
  • Mohawk offers onsite clinic access to membership around the country.  Customers can locate an onsite clinic by going to the Mohawk benefits page -- Healthy Life Centers – Mohawk Benefits.
  • The HLC Care Teams include medical providers, Healthy Life Navigators, case managers, referral coordinators, registered dietitians, and behavioral health specialists.  
  • The clinics offer primary care, acute care, preventive services, lab services, chronic condition management, coaching, and assistance with care coordination for referrals, procedures, and screenings. 
  • The charge for acute and primary care visits to the HLCs is $25, subject to plan provisions (CYD, coinsurance and OOP).  Other services by the HLCs for chronic care management and preventive services are at no cost to the customer.
