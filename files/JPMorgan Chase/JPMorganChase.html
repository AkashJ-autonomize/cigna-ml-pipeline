    • Specialized EAC Procedures
      • All Special protocols are noted below.
    • Note: JPMC has requested that we help manage the number of hours scheduled for CISM requests. They have implemented the following “Approval” process (Click Here):
      • Any CISM Request for general Stress Management that is not directly related to an on-site robbery or traumatic event should be referred to the Internal EAP Core Team at EAP_CORE_TEAM@chase.com with subject Line “Stress Management CISM Request” someone from the Internal EAP Core Teamwill assess the situation and align other resources to support the branch.
      • CISM requests: The standard is usually 2 hours unless it is something catastrophic or extremely traumatic (Click Here):
        • For example if it was an incident with lesser impact such as a note passer robbery or death of a coworker with a small work group 2 hours should be sufficient.
        • We should direct the conversation about hours rather than start by asking how many hours they want: “We find for a situation like this 2 hours is usually sufficient. We will follow-up with you and discuss further needs.”
        • If the manager does not agree on 2 hours or less the EAC should explain that we then need to get approval from the Internal EAP Core Team.
        • If the manager asks how long that will take, we can say 30 minutes to an hour and we always have the option to start with the 2 hours.
        • The EAC has flexibility to use their clinical discretion, but clearly document in the case why.
        • If there was a Disaster or other highly traumatic situation, takeover robbery, shooting, etc… this process does not apply. EAC can go ahead and schedule the appropriate number of hours without approval. They want us to use our own judgement.
        • If we schedule 2 hours and learn the Provider was asked to stay longer because people were still asking to meet we can go ahead and use our discretion to approve those hours.
        • If in our follow-up we feel more hours are needed and have a good clinical basis for this we do not need approval.
        • If in our follow-up they are asking for more hours and we see not real clinical need then approval is needed.
      • CISM Debriefing Approval process (Click Here):
        • Send email to the Internal EAP Core Team at EAP_CORE_TEAM@chase.com with Subject Line “Request for Longer CISM”. In the body of the email indicate the branch address, manager’s name, phone, and email address along with what happened, date, and manager’s reason for wanting/needing more than 2 hours.
        • the Internal EAP Core Team will attempt to give approval in 30 mins, if not, we can start with 2 hours and work with the provider to extend if The Internal EAP Core Team approves more.
        • If the Internal EAP Core Team declines the request the EAC needs to notify the manager that it was declined and that they will receive an email from their Internal EAP (Core Team) with the rationale.
        • We can still authorize the 2 hours.
      • Please outreach Renee Webster with questions on the process
