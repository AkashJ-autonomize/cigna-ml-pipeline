EAP:  
Behavioral:
  • OAP Carve-in
  • Fully Insured (Guaranteed Cost)
  • Standard 30/60 Transition
  • Tufts Health Plan pays all Behavioral claims
  • This is an Alliance offering where THP is the Coordinating Party. Cigna Behavioral Health will conduct UM for those members accessing care outside MA & RI (using CBH providers)
  • Tufts Health Plan and Cigna are in a strategic Alliance together (Carelink). To determine whether Cigna needs to manage the case, first look at the customer’s member ID.
    • If the Member ID begins with a U, Cigna will always manage the case. 
    • If the Member ID begins with a 7, 8 or 9, and treatment is outside of MA or RI, Cigna will manage the case.
    • If the Member ID begins with a 7, 8, or 9, and treatment is inside of MA or RI, please refer to Tufts as Cigna does not manage this.
  • CBH has no claims liability. All claims inquiries should be directed to the customer service number at Tufts Health Plan 800.208.9565
