Additional Info:
  • Performance Guarantees
    • Documented Care Plan Closed Case
      •  At least 80% of applicable case management cases (those with at least 2 case management contacts) will have a documented care plan in place (at a minimum, the case plan must identify one (1) short term goal, and one (1) long term goal).   
    • High Cost CM
      • 90% of cases exceeding $50,000 will be assessed and 85% will receive intervention ( i.e telephonic outreach, development of a treatment plan, referral to an appropriate program).   
    • Post-Discharge
      • Cigna will make no fewer than 3 attempts (via call, or letter, or e-mail) within 5 business days of discharge (excluding the discharge date) on callable phone numbers to reach 90% of participants/caregivers. This applies to customers who have had a hospital length of stay equal to or greater than 1 day.  
    • Pre-Admit
      • Cigna will make 3 attempts (via call, or letter) to reach 90% of patients with valid phone numbers scheduled for a non-maternity IP admission when notified 4 Business Days prior to admission.  
    • Satisfaction
      • Health Matters Care Management Satisfaction. At least 85% of survey responses received from individuals participating in a Cigna HealthCare Health Matters Care Management Program will indicate satisfaction with their case management experience.  One year Guarantee Period.
