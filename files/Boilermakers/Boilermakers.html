  • C. Professional Claims (Physician or Practitioner)
    • 1.0  Professional Services not otherwise specified in MSA
      Charges for professional services are based upon the primary diagnosis code billed. Services with behavioral health diagnoses are the responsibility of CBH.
      Services with medical diagnoses are the responsibility of the Fund. This rule applies to inpatient services, outpatient services, consultations, testing, etc.
    • Exceptions: 
      I.  Behavioral health provider billing with Neuropsychiatric Testing codes are administered by The Fund.  All Neuropsychiatric testing codes are the responsibility of The Fund
      II. Behavioral health provider (PhD’s) billing for Health and Behavioral Assessment services with medical DX codes are administered by The Fund.  Procedure Codes include 96150, 96151, 96152, 96153, 96154, 96155
