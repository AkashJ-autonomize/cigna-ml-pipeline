Employees that enrolled their new or unverified dependent to BJC medical, dental or vision coverage, and/or enrolled or changed benefit elections based on a qualified life event (e.g., marriage, divorce, birth), will be required to provide documentation to support dependent eligibility and/or documentation to support the qualified life event.
